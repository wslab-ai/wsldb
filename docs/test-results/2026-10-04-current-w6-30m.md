## Workload

The frozen W6 mix ran for 1,800 seconds over 1,000,000 keys and
deterministic incompressible 100-byte values. It used the W5 operation mix of
80% point reads, 15% durable overwrites, and 5% durable delete/recreate cycles,
but selected keys from the fixed rejection-inversion Zipf distribution with
exponent 1.2. WSLDB used a 256 MiB decoded-page cache and a 64 MiB durable
write buffer. Every mutation requested synchronous WAL durability. Every 60
active windows the harness performed a full verification and logical hash
outside the active interval. The final drain consolidated and synced the WAL
before another full verification.
