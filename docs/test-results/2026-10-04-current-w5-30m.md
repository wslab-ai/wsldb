## Workload

The frozen W5 mix ran for 1,800 seconds over 1,000,000 keys and
deterministic incompressible 100-byte values. It used 80% point reads, 15%
durable overwrites, and 5% durable delete/recreate cycles. WSLDB used a 256 MiB
decoded-page cache and a 64 MiB durable write buffer. Every mutation requested
synchronous WAL durability. Every 60 active windows the harness performed a
full verification and logical hash outside the active interval. The final
drain consolidated and synced the WAL before another full verification.
